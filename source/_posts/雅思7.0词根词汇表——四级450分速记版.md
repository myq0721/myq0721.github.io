## 怎么用这张表

1. **先记词根义**，再记前缀（`re-` 再、`de-` 向下/去除、`inter-` 之间……），最后看整词。
2. **每天 2–3 个词根**，用「词根 + 例句」复习，不要只盯中文释义。
3. 标了 **R** 的词阅读高频，**W** 写作好用，**L** 听力常考——优先记带标记的。
4. 四级已学过的词（如 `transport`、`describe`）当作锚点，往同族词上挂新词。

**串联短文**

In **retrospect**, most people **respected** the city's **prospect** for growth, yet one **suspicious** man aroused **suspicion** and became a **suspect** in a minor theft. Witnesses said they **despised** his rude manner, which ruined their **perspective** on an otherwise joyful afternoon.
